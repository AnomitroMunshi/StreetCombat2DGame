# V0.3 Complete Combat Evolution: audit against the milestone spec

**Audited:** branch `v0.3-combat-evolution` @ `7f058fa`, clean tree. All 7 headless test suites pass. Everything below was checked in the source.

**Legend:** ✅ done (code and tests) · 🟡 partial · ❌ missing

## M0 Audit and safety ✅

- Work happens on a dedicated branch, one commit per batch, and the branch is pushed.
- Tests live in `tests/` and run through `tests/run_all.sh` (an import/parse check plus 6 suites).

## M1 Combat state and collision 🟡

**Done (tests in `test_core`, `test_flow`, `test_match`, `test_ai_finisher`):**
- **Match lifecycle:** PRE_MATCH → … → RESULT is tick-driven. A restart reloads the scene.
- **One hit per swing:** each swing can connect once, and only during its active frames.
- **Facing:** attacks are symmetric left and right.
- **Boxes:** pushbox (60 px minimum spacing), hurtbox and hitbox are kept separate.
- **Rules:** trades and double K.O. are allowed, the finisher runs once, and the result waits until the loser's body is at rest.

**Missing:** the developer overlay draws the pushbox, hurtbox and hitbox, but not the **rendered fist/foot position** or the **actual contact point**. → Batch 1.

## M2 Animation 🟡

**Done:**
- Keyframe timelines with startup, active and recovery phases (S/A/R).
- Contact-aware reach: strikes stop on the body instead of passing through.
- Distinct hit reactions.
- Rear-heel pivot on the cross and uppercut.
- Sweep following the reference sheet.
- Back kick with a body turn.
- Ragdoll and get-up.

**Polish still possible:**

- Jump attack landing.

## M3 Active idle ✅

- Each stance has its own loop.
- Each fighter has its own tempo and phase.
- Feet stay planted.
- The idle fades with actions and with posture.
- The root never moves.

## M4 Responsiveness and skill 🟡

**Done:**
- Parry: a 4-tick window, whiff recovery, a cooldown, low parry, and a stagger on the attacker.
- Posture, guard break, and a lock-out so breaks can't loop.
- Explicit combo branches; a whiffed attack can't chain.
- Juggle cap.
- Perfect dodge.
- AI parry and punish.

**Missing:**
- **Counter hits** add posture but no **enhanced hitstun**, and have no distinct sound. → Batch 2.
- **Clash feedback** when two active strikes collide. → Batch 2.

## M5 Start match ✅

- Button and countdown, with input and AI locked until FIGHT.
- Both sides unlock on the same tick.
- Mashing the button and restarting are safe.
- A restart clears effects.
