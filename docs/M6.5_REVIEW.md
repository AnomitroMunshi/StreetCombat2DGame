## Deviations and decisions

- **Wall Crush strikes come from the style.** The spec says to preserve Wall Crush with a flying knee. A playtest found a boxer kneeing (and earlier kicking), which breaks style identity. The wall, cracks, chunks, pin and slump are unchanged and shared by every style. Only the strikes change, through the style's `wall_entry` field (5 poses). Muay Thai keeps the flying knee. To restore the spec's universal knee, clear `wall_entry` in the style files.
- **Low kicks are low attacks.** The Muay Thai and Kickboxing low kick and the Kung Fu low side kick must be blocked low (S+Shift). This gives a real defence against them.
- **Signatures earn no Focus,** and neither do signature-sequence strikes. Only the last strike of a sequence can knock down, so the whole combo can land.
- **New AI behaviour: recovery punish.** Found in testing: short-range rivals (Boxing, Kung Fu) never landed a hit. Rivals now sometimes punish an opponent who is stuck in recovery within reach. This uses observable state only.

## Manual playtest checklist (needs a human)

1. **Style screen.** Cards, details and the live guard and idle preview. Cycle with Q, confirm with ENTER or a click. Check readability at your resolution.
2. **Each style for 2–3 minutes,** facing both directions:
   - every key in the style's move list does what the list says;
   - no move from another style ever comes out (try every key, in the air too);
   - the stance and idle feel distinct.
3. **Feel of the new moves.** Do these read as their technique?
   - elbow and knee range;
   - axe kick and spinning hook kick;
   - reverse punch and knife hand;
   - palm chains and deflection counter;
   - low kicks.
4. **Focus.** Is the gain rate right? A full meter takes about 10–15 clean actions. Does each signature feel strong but punishable? The Breaker Combination and Palm Barrage should be interrupted when you hit the user mid-sequence.
5. **Finishers.** Win on open floor several times with each style; each of its two finishers should appear. Check Wall Crush near a pillar with a non-Muay-Thai style, and Air Smash with an uppercut or rising-strike K.O.
6. **Rival.** Fight several rival styles. Do they feel different, and do they use their signature now and then? None should feel unbeatable or helpless.
7. **Flow.** Check REMATCH (same style), CHANGE STYLE (back to selection), R mid-countdown, and R mid-finisher.
